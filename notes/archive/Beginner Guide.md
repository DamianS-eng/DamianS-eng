# How I feel about playing the Beginner's Guide

At first, I'd believe the narrator about his diving into his friend's games, being concerned about their meaning . But a couple of odd things that strike me are his obsession with playing more of his games and finding a solution in games that are meant to go on forever. He mentions having debates to Coda about how the meanings are conveyed, but with how in-depth he knows about the games, are they really Coda's or his own creations? Is Coda fictional?
Or was Coda really depressed?
Are the elements that Davey added at ease because he's so knowledgeable about his games, or does he know enough to make his own work?
