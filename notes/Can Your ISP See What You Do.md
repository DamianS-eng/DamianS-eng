# Yes

# But What Do They Really See?

- Visited websites & domains
- Time of visit
- Duration
- Amount of data transferred
  - Xfinity, especially, gauges this amount to limit home Internet usersto 1TB a month of data.
- Estimate of physical location
- Devices connected to home network
## How?

- DNS Requests
- Connection metadata
- Network infrastructure
  - ISPs provide rentals of network equipment and have the means to access all data, settings and infrastructure with it

## Wi-Fi sensing

Wi-Fi signals can be used to detect movement by analyzing how wireless signals reflect off people and objects inside buildings.

> Track movement, breathing patterns, and estimate body positions through walls. 
## HTTPS

Prevents the ISP from reading the contents of encrypted webpages.

# It All Comes Together

ISPs offer in addition:

- Mobile phone services
- Television platforms
- Email services
- Smart home devices
- Cloud services
- Search products

and they use all these services in conjunction with Internet data to build a detailed advertising profile for each of their customers.
